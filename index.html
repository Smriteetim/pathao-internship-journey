Project spotlight

0% Commission Subscription Campaign

Goal

Make riders aware of the 0% Commission Subscription Package

Bike and taxi riders can subscribe to a package and ride without paying commission per trip. The campaign’s job was to explain this and get riders to activate it.

My role

From script to screen to report

  • Scripts and content for rider testimonial and how-to video ads, including a step-by-step guide to 0% commission
  • Coordinated shoots and creatives with the content and design teams
  • Monitored delivery, reach and cost in Meta Ads Manager
